When specifying a magnetic lighting system, the fixture is only part of the equation.
Long-term reliability, optical consistency, installation efficiency, and system flexibility ultimately determine the performance of a project.
At ENCORE, we developed our 48V Magnetic Lighting Platform from an engineering perspective rather than simply expanding a product range.
A platform designed for architectural and technical lighting
Instead of offering isolated luminaires, our system combines:
20mm/10mm/5mm track profile for different installations: pendant, surface and recessed
Spotlights, framing projector, linear wall washers, pendants and decorative modules
A complete range of connectors and accessories
This allows designers to create multiple lighting effects while maintaining a consistent architectural language throughout the project.
Engineered for long-term reliability
Many magnetic systems look similar—but their internal engineering is not.
ENCORE's system is designed for durability and stable performance through details that matter:
High-purity copper strip conductors with a larger contact surface for stable conductivity
Thick 3 mm track housing for improved structural stability
Dust-protected track design that helps maintain reliable electrical contact over time
Polarity-free wiring to simplify installation and reduce wiring errors
Safety-lock magnetic adapters with quick-release design for secure installation and easy maintenance
These engineering details help reduce maintenance while ensuring consistent performance throughout the system's lifetime.
