In retail lighting, the best spotlight should attract attention to the merchandise — not to itself.
That is the idea behind EVO Track Light.
1. Minimal in form. Made to blend in.
EVO features an exceptionally slim cylindrical body with an integrated driver, eliminating the visual bulk often seen with conventional track spotlights.
Finished in timeless black or white with carefully refined details, it blends naturally into different retail interiors — keeping the ceiling clean while allowing the merchandise and architecture to take visual priority.
2. Light exactly where it matters.
Precise optics create a clean, controlled beam, while the deep anti-glare design keeps direct glare away from the eye.
With UGR <16, or even <10 with the honeycomb filter, EVO delivers strong accent lighting without sacrificing visual comfort — helping products stand out while creating a more pleasant shopping environment.
3. One fixture, 3 colour temperatures.
A built-in switch allows easy selection between 2700K / 3000K / 4000K(or customized CCT),  this gives retailers and lighting partners greater flexibility to match different merchandise, materials and store atmospheres — while reducing the number of CCT SKUs that need to be stocked and managed.
EVO is designed around a simple idea:less visual noise from the luminaire, more attention on what you want customers to see!
