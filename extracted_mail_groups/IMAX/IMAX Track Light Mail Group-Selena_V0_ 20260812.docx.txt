Mail 2: IMAX-Zoom
Subject
1. From narrow 6° to Wide Flood 70°—Without Changing the Luminaire 
2. IMAX Zoomable: One Fixture for a Changing Exhibition 
3. The Beam Changes. The Gallery Stays Ready.
Email Body
Dear [First Name], 
Exhibitions change. Artwork dimensions, display positions and lighting distances change with them.
With fixed-beam spotlights, every change may require a different optic or even a different luminaire. IMAX Zoomable turns each spotlight into a flexible commissioning tool. 
1. From an ultra-narrow 6° accent to wide illumination 
The continuously adjustable optical system starts from an exceptionally narrow 6° beam, allowing designers to isolate a small artefact with precision. The beam can then be opened smoothly for larger paintings, sculptures or display areas—without stepped adjustments or optic replacement. 
2. Individual dimming down to 1% 
A built-in individual dimmer allows the output of each spotlight to be adjusted directly and smoothly down to 1%. Dark paintings and light-sensitive works on paper can therefore share the same track while receiving very different light levels—making on-site balancing faster and more precise. 
3. Complete aiming freedom in a compact form 
The clean cylindrical body offers 360°+ horizontal rotation and 90° vertical adjustment, allowing light to reach virtually any position in the space. Its stable mechanical structure keeps the spotlight accurately aimed after commissioning.
The result is fewer fixture variants, faster exhibition changes and greater control over every illuminated object—all within the restrained IMAX design language.

Mail 3: IMAX-Projector
Subject
1. Make the Light Stop at the Artwork 
2. IMAX Projector: Precision Has an Edge
Email Body
Dear [First Name], 
In museum & exhibition lighting, precision is not only about where the light falls. It is equally about where the light stops. 
IMAX-Projector gives lighting designers the control to define that boundary—shaping light around the exhibit instead of allowing it to spill onto the surrounding wall or display case.
1. Shape light to the exact form of the exhibit 
Four independently adjustable framing blades create square, rectangular and irregular light fields. The focus can be adjusted from a crisp, high-contrast outline to a softer transition, allowing the light effect to suit both graphic and atmospheric exhibition concepts. The high-transmission optical system produces a clean, uniform field with low distortion, keeping attention on the artwork rather than on optical artefacts.
2. From a 3° micro-accent to a 45° light field 
The adjustable iris provides a beam range from 3° to 45°. At its narrowest, IMAX can isolate a piece of jewellery or a small artefact inside a display case with minimal spill. Opened wider, it can frame paintings and larger exhibition objects with the same controlled precision. 
3. From precise presentation to creative scenography 
Interchangeable filters and customised logo or pattern projection extend IMAX beyond conventional artwork illumination. Designers can introduce colour, symbols or visual guidance—turning the projector into a flexible tool for storytelling and spatial orientation. 
With 360°+ rotation and 90° vertical adjustment, every effect can be positioned precisely and held securely after commissioning. 
The result is light that does not simply illuminate an exhibit. It defines its presence, strengthens visual hierarchy and lets the surrounding architecture recede.
