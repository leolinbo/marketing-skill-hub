In dynamic retail, gallery, museum, and hospitality spaces, displays and layouts change—but conventional track lights still require manual adjustment at ceiling height. This increases setup time, disrupts operations, and makes precise lighting scenes difficult to reproduce.
MOVIA motorized track light turns these repeated on-site adjustments into simple, app-based control—helping teams commission faster, adapt spaces more easily, and maintain consistent lighting from one setup to the next.
How MOVIA solves it?
1. Complete lighting control—from the Casambi app
From floor level, users can remotely adjust 0–100% dimming, color temperature from 2700K to 6000K, 360° pan and 140° tilt. With the Zoomable version, beam angle is adjusted from 18° to 60° in the CASAMBI app. No ladders, no fixture-by-fixture manual focusing—just fast, intuitive control from one interface.
2. Two optical versions for two different needs
Choose the Zoomable 18°–60° version when displays, object sizes, or lighting moods change frequently. Choose the Fixed 9° version when a concentrated, precise beam is required for artwork, merchandise, or dramatic focal lighting.
3. Flexible control and mounting for real project conditions
Group luminaires, save preset scenes, and recall complete lighting settings in seconds—ideal for spaces that serve different displays, events, or layouts. Track-Mounted and Monopoint options provide additional flexibility, making MOVIA easier to integrate into different ceiling plans and on-site commissioning requirements.
4. Fast, ultra-quiet, and engineered for reliable operation
High-speed precision motors respond quickly while operating at ultra-low noise. Stable, repeatable positioning helps each saved scene return accurately—providing dependable performance in galleries, museums, premium retail, and other noise-sensitive interiors.
