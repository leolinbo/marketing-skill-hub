In galleries, museums, and premium retail spaces, displays change frequently. Yet fixed beam angles and preset light settings often force a compromise: the beam is too wide or too narrow, the CCT does not suit the displayed material, or the brightness is difficult to balance consistently.
OVEGA-Zoom with digital screen brings 4 essential adjustments into one track spotlight. CCT and brightness are set with the rear rotary/push control and shown numerically on the digital display, while beam angle and aiming direction are adjusted manually at the luminaire.
1. Set and verify the exact CCT and brightness
    Tunable white: 2700–6000 K in precise 50 K steps; press the knob to jump quickly to commonly used CCT presets.
    Fine dimming: 0.1–100% in 0.1% steps; press the knob to switch quickly between 0%, 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100%.
   Customer value: faster on-site tuning, clear visual confirmation of CCT and brightness, and settings that are easier to repeat from one spotlight to another.   
2. Match the beam to every display
   The beam is adjusted through a stepless manual zoom, from 25° spot to 60° flood, allowing the beam to fit a small object, a large artwork, or a product display without changing the luminaire or optic. The result is cleaner emphasis with less unwanted light spill.
 
3. Aim light exactly where it is needed
   Direction is adjusted manually at the spotlight. With 360° rotation and 90° tilt, OVEGA adapts easily to changing layouts, different mounting positions, and demanding aiming requirements.
By combining digitally displayed CCT and brightness settings with manual zoom and flexible aiming, OVEGA-Zoom with digital screen helps designers and operators fine-tune every display on site—reducing setup time while maintaining clean focus and consistent presentation as exhibitions or merchandise change.
