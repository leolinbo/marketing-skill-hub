In museums, galleries and premium retail, display layouts rarely remain fixed. Artwork changes, merchandise moves, and each object requires a different beam, brightness and aiming angle. When every lighting task needs a different luminaire, specification becomes complex and future adjustments become costly.
MEGA Track Light Family is designed to solve this challenge: one square track spotlight family that adapts from precise accent lighting to floodlighting and wallwashing - while maintaining a consistent visual language across the ceiling.
Match the beam to the object.     Choose 10°, 25°, 40° or 60° lenses for anything from a small, high-contrast accent to broad illumination.     A Magical 10° lens can also be combined with tool-free snap-on filters to create different distributions: spotlights, floodlights and wallwashing. Exchange the filter on site instead of replacing the luminaire. The lighting team can reshape the beam quickly as artworks, merchandise or layouts are updated.
Set the right light level.      Different materials and viewing conditions need different brightness. Except control options including Triac, DALI, Casambi, also support On-board dimmer(0/1-10V dimming), enabling precise adjustment for each display.
Aim with precision.    A 90° tilt and 360°+ rotation make it easy to direct light exactly where required. CRI 90+/95+ options and 3-step SDCM support faithful, consistent colour appearance across the installation. 
The result is fewer luminaire types to manage, faster on-site adjustment and a lighting system that can evolve with the display.
Its slim cubic design also offers a distinctive alternative to conventional cylindrical spotlights - particularly suited to galleries, museums and design-led retail spaces.
If you are working on a project, share the ceiling height, track layout and key exhibit dimensions with us. We will recommend the most suitable MEGA optics and control configuration.
