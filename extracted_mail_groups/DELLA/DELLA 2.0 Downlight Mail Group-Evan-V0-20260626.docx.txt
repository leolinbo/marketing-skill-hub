A Unified Family for the Whole Project
From Mini, Adjustable, Fixed, Wall Washer, Pinhole to IP65, DELLA 2.0 allows different lighting functions to be specified within one consistent design language.

Consistent Light Quality, From Spot to Wall Wash
DELLA 2.0 is engineered for refined optical performance - smooth beam distribution, excellent glare control, and clean wall washing without unwanted shadows or dark areas.
