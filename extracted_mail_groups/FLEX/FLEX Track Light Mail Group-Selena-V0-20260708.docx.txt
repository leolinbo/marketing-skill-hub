For retail stores, galleries, showrooms and commercial interiors, cylinder spotlights are always a clean and timeless choice.
But what if a classic cylinder could also be foldable, adjustable and visually distinctive — while still delivering precise optical performance and efficient heat management?
That is why we developed FLEX Track Light.
FLEX keeps the simplicity of a cylindrical spotlight, but adds a foldable joint structure for greater aiming flexibility and a more unique architectural expression.
Available options:
2 sizes: 55mm / 10W, 76mm / 15–25W
2 optical solutions: Fixed beam(15°, 24°, 38°, 55°) and zoomable(15-40°)
2 installation methods: Track-mounted and surface-mounted
Why Choose FLEX?
1. Precise Optical Control for Professional Accent Lighting
FLEX is designed for accurate beam control in professional accent lighting applications. Whether using the fixed beam or zoomable version, it helps designers highlight products, feature walls, artworks and architectural details with clarity. The integrated anti-glare optical structure and optional honeycomb filter further improve visual comfort, making FLEX suitable for retail, gallery and hospitality environments where light quality directly affects the customer experience.
2. Foldable Cylinder Design with Refined Details
FLEX reinterprets the classic cylinder spotlight with a foldable structural design. When closed, the magnetic connection brings the LED engine and driver section together into a clean, complete cylinder form; when opened, it becomes a flexible directional spotlight with a distinctive architectural character. This gives FLEX both a timeless minimalist appearance and a more expressive design identity for modern commercial interiors.
3. Flexible Structure for Different Ceiling Conditions
FLEX supports both track-mounted and surface-mounted applications, allowing designers to maintain one consistent product language across different ceiling conditions. With 360° rotation and 90° vertical tilt, the light can be aimed precisely without dead angles, making it ideal for spaces where displays, furniture layouts or visual focal points may change over time.
If you are looking for a spotlight that combines classic cylinder aesthetics, precise light control and flexible adjustment, FLEX can be a strong solution for your next retail or commercial project.
